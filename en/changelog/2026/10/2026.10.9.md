## New Features

### ElevenLabs voice catalog

On **Voices**, the **Catalog** tab lists ElevenLabs voices to search, filter, and preview. **Use this voice** saves the preset under **My voices**, ready for chat and the AI Agent.

Audio generated with that voice spends AI credits. The catalog sample does not.

| Models | Price |
|--------|--------|
| Flash v2.5, v4 Turbo, and v3 Conversational | US$ 0.13 per 1K characters |
| v4, v3, and Multilingual v2 | US$ 0.26 per 1K characters |

Without balance, audio is not generated. Voices tied to your own integration still use that key.

> Full guide: [System voices](/en/guide/chat/system-voices#interflow-catalog)

---

## Benefits

- Pick a voice without leaving Interflow
- Usage stays on the AI credit balance
- Each model’s price shows when you save the voice
