## New Features

### Profile photo

Each person on the team can add a photo to their own account. The image shows in the side menu, in conversations, on the member list, and wherever the name was already shown. Anyone who has not uploaded a photo still appears with the initial of their name.

#### What is it for?
- Recognize who is speaking or serving without reading the full name
- Make the team easier to identify day to day

#### How to use it
1. Open **Profile** from your name in the side menu
2. Tap **Choose picture** and pick an image from your device
3. The photo is adjusted automatically and applies right away
4. To replace it, use **Change picture**. The previous image is replaced
5. To go back to the initial, use **Remove picture**

---

## Benefits

- The team recognizes each other faster in conversations and lists
- Anyone who prefers not to upload a photo stays identified by the initial of their name
