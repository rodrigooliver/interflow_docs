## New Features

### Call on WhatsApp from the appointment

Each schedule can show a shortcut that opens the user's own WhatsApp on the customer's saved number, outside Interflow's support channel.

#### What it is for
- Confirm a time, send instructions, or arrange a change without looking up the phone on another screen
- Start the chat on the person's own WhatsApp, not on the integrated channel

#### How to use
1. When editing the schedule, turn on **Direct customer WhatsApp**
2. On the list, the calendar, or the appointment actions menu, use **Call on WhatsApp**
3. WhatsApp opens in a new tab with **Hello, {customer name}** already filled in

The shortcut uses the WhatsApp contact and, if there is none, the phone. The number must include the country code and, with symbols removed, have 10 to 15 digits. Without a valid number, the button stays hidden.

> Guide: [Schedule — Call on WhatsApp](/en/guide/schedule/#call-on-whatsapp)

## Improvements

### Meet only when the schedule creates the meeting

The meeting-link block on the appointment, and the Meet menu on the card, appear only when the schedule has **Create a Google Meet when the appointment is created**. If the appointment already has a saved link, it stays available to open or copy.

---

## Benefits

- Day-to-day contact starts from the appointment itself, outside the integrated inbox
- The opening message already includes the customer's name
- Schedules that do not use Google Meet no longer show the meeting block
