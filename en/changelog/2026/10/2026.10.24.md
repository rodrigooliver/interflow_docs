## New Features

### Appointments for the professional only

On the **Appointments** menu, anyone without the **See all appointments** permission sees only the bookings where they are the professional. The list, the calendar, and a direct appointment link follow that rule.

#### What it is for
- Each person focuses on their own visits
- They cannot open another professional’s schedule
- Owner, admin, and medical assistant still see the clinic schedule
- Manager, agent, limited agent, sales, and doctor see only their own

#### How to use
1. Open **Appointments**
2. If the permission is off, the filter stays on **My appointments**
3. To open the full schedule for a member who belongs to a group, open their permissions, on the group tab, and check **See all appointments**
4. The group still applies to everything else; only this exception is individual

Bookings with no professional do not appear for people who see only their own.

> Guide: [Schedule — Who sees appointments](/en/guide/schedule/#who-sees-appointments)

## Benefits

- Each professional’s schedule stays private
- The exception is granted without removing the person from their permission group
