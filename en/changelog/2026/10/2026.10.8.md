## New Features

### Company away messages

The organization now has its own **Away messages** screen in settings, below Teams and before Inbox preferences.

#### What it is for
- Set the default individual away text, used when an agent goes offline and has not saved their own message
- Create several company rules, each with its own text, channels, status, flow, and hours
- Reply on waiting or in-progress chats, including chats with no agent, when the individual away message does not apply

#### How to use
1. Open **Settings** and go to **Away messages**
2. On the individual card, edit the default text and save
3. Under company rules, add or edit a rule in the modal
4. Pick the channels you created (search and multi-select), the status, and whether a flow is running
5. Pick the time zone and build hours day by day: each weekday is added once, with that day's ranges
6. Save. List order is priority: the first matching rule is sent

If the chat's agent is offline with customer notice on, their message takes priority over the company rule. The same chat does not get another automatic away message for 30 minutes.

## Improvements

### Automatic customer deletion

When usage hits the plan limit, deletion no longer looks only at the registration date. The queue takes old registrations that also have no recent conversation. Anyone who chatted recently stays, even with an old registration.

---

## Benefits

- The customer gets a reply outside business hours or when the team is away
- The agent's text and the company text can differ
- Each channel and each weekday can have its own rule
- Customers in recent contact are not removed only because their registration is old
