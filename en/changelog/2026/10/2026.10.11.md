## New Features

### Gemini for conversation and voices

Google Gemini is available for conversation and text-to-speech. The same key serves the agent and voices. Without a key, catalog models use the AI credit balance.

#### What it is for

- Answer with Gemini models on the agent, prompt test, and copilot
- Generate audio with 30 built-in voices and five TTS models
- Hear a sample with the chosen text and model before using the voice in chat

#### How to use it

1. Under **Settings** → **Integrations**, save a [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey) key, or use Interflow credits
2. On the agent, pick the Gemini integration and the model. Under **Parameters**, set the reasoning level (**Low**, **Medium**, or **High**) and the max tokens. Temperature does not apply to these models
3. Under **Voices** → **Catalog**, open **Gemini** or **OpenAI**, click **Test text** and, if you want, change the model inside the test. **Use this voice** saves it on the Interflow balance. The OpenAI default is GPT-4o Mini TTS
4. Edit the suggested text and generate. The file is MP3 and spends credits when the voice uses the Interflow balance
5. **Use this voice** saves the preset under **My voices**. The same test is on the card and in the form, including ElevenLabs

> Full guide: [Gemini integration](/en/guide/integrations/gemini) · [System voices](/en/guide/chat/system-voices#gemini)

## Benefits

- Gemini conversation and voice on the same key, or on the credit balance
- A sample with the chosen model and text before sending audio to the customer
- The file arrives as MP3, ready for the conversation
- In the copilot, the Gemini reply streams in, and the reasoning summary follows the low, medium, or high level
