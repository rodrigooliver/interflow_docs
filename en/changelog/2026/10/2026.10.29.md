## New Features

### Import a statement

Under **Financial → Entries → Import**, a bank CSV or OFX lands on the register after a line-by-line review.

#### What it's for
- Bring the statement in without retyping date, description, and amount
- Decide, on each line, whether to create an entry, settle an open item, or skip
- Register on the spot a category that came in the file and does not exist yet

#### How to use it
1. Choose the register, download the sample CSV if you need the template, then upload the file
2. For CSV, map date, description, and amount (or debit and credit). Category and cost center are optional. OFX skips this step
3. On review, each line has its own columns for category, cost center, payment method, and customer
4. The defaults at the top apply income category, expense category, cost center, and payment method to new lines. The category list is a tree you can search
5. If the category name from the file does not exist, the line shows **Not registered**. **Register** creates the category and fills the other matching lines that were still empty
6. Confirm. The summary reports how many lines were created, settled, and skipped

A category is required to create a line. Customer, payment method, and cost center are optional. Without the chosen open item, the settlement is not saved.

> Full guide: [Financial — Import a statement](/en/guide/financial/import)

---

## Benefits

- A bank statement becomes register entries, with a review before anything is saved
- Category, payment method, and customer sit side by side, so the mapping does not wrap onto extra lines
- A new category from the file can be registered once and applied to every line with the same name
