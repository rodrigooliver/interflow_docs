## New Features

### Filter by last conversation status

On the customer list, the filter uses the same status as the **Last conversation** column. The list shows only people who match the selection.

#### What is it for?
- See who is waiting for a reply or already being served
- Separate finished conversations or those waiting to close
- Find customers with no conversation

#### How to use it?
1. In **Customers**, open filters
2. Under **Last conversation**, choose **Filter by status**
3. Select one or more: Waiting, In progress, Finished, Waiting to close, or **No data or no conversation**
4. Apply. List export uses the same filter

> Guide: [Customers — Last conversation status](/en/guide/crm/customers#last-conversation-status)

---

## Benefits

- The team prioritizes the queue without scanning the whole list
- Customers who never had a conversation show up in their own filter
