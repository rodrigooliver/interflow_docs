## New Features

### Average response time by agent

In **Reports**, a manager sees each agent's average response time for the selected period. The team average on the dashboard stays; this table shows each person.

#### What is it for?
- See who replies faster and who is slower
- Compare today, the last 7 days, the last 30 days, or a custom range
- Follow individual service without relying only on the team average

#### How to use it
1. In the menu, open **Reports**
2. Choose **Average response time**
3. Select the period
4. The table lists the agent's name and the average for that range

The figure uses only manual team replies. Anyone with no reply in the period is left out of the table.

> Guide: [Reports — Average response time](/en/guide/reports/#average-response-time)

- Individual performance sits with the other reports
