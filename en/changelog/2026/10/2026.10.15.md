## New Features

### Ad origin in the AI Agent

When someone reaches WhatsApp from a Facebook or Instagram ad, the flow AI Agent already knows which ad they came from.

The context includes the title, a short excerpt of the text, the app, and the ad type. Click identifiers and media links stay out of the conversation with the AI.

#### What it is for
- Reply around the ad the person clicked
- Handle a paid-campaign lead without the team restating the source
- Keep the conversation aligned with the ad message

#### How to use it
There is no new setting. The data arrives on the first WhatsApp message (official or WAHA). If the customer already has that origin saved, the agent uses the profile. If not, it uses the first message.
