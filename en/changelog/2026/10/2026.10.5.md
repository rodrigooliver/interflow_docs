## New Features

### Google Meet on the appointment

The agent creates the Google Meet from the appointment itself. The link is saved and the customer conversation receives the join message.

#### What it is for
- Start a video call without leaving the schedule
- Keep the link on the booking to open or copy later
- Reuse the same link in schedule reminders

#### How to use
1. Connect Google Calendar in Settings, on the organization integration
2. On a saved appointment, use **Create Meet** on the card or in the form. The system creates the meeting, stores the link, and sends it in the linked conversation
3. If the link already exists, the button resends the message. Without a linked conversation, the link stays saved so you can copy it
4. On the schedule, turn on **Create Google Meet when the appointment is created** so new bookings get a link on their own. Editing or rescheduling does not create another meeting
5. In schedule message templates, use `{{meet_link}}` for the address

> Guide: [Schedule — Google Meet](/en/guide/schedule/#google-meet)

## Benefits

- The customer gets the right link in the conversation, without a separate meeting setup
- Later reminders can repeat the same address
