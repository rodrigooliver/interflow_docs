## New Features

### Delete selected messages

Admins and owners can select several messages in a conversation and delete them at once, only inside Interflow.

The message leaves the team's history. It stays on the connected channel, such as WhatsApp.

#### What it is for
- Clean a long history without deleting one message at a time
- Remove from the conversation what the team no longer needs to see
- Keep what the customer already received in their app

#### How to use
1. In the conversation menu, choose **Select messages**
2. Check the messages
3. Click **Delete selected**
4. Confirm

The button is available only to admins and owners. Deleting a single message from its menu stays as before and may also try to delete it on the channel.

> Guide: [Chat — Delete selected messages](/en/guide/chat/interface#delete-selected-messages)

---

## Benefits

- Cleaning a long conversation fits in one confirmation
- The team's history stays tidy without changing what was already sent on the channel
- Agents still do not get this bulk delete
