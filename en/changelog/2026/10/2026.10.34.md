## New Features

### Nickname for an official template

Each WhatsApp Official API template can get a nickname or a short description, only so the team can recognize the model inside Interflow. The official name and the text sent to the customer stay the same.

#### What is it for?
- Find the right template when the Meta name is unclear or looks like another
- See the nickname on cards, in search, in a flow, in a bulk send, and when creating a conversation

#### How to use it
1. Open the **WhatsApp Business (Official)** channel and go to **Templates**
2. When creating or editing, fill in **Nickname or description** (optional, up to 120 characters)
3. Save. If only the nickname changed, the template is not sent back for approval
4. In the official-channel chat, type `/` and search by nickname or official name. Choosing the item opens that template for sending

> Full guide: [WhatsApp Templates](/en/guide/channels/whatsapp-templates#nickname)

---

## Benefits

- The team finds the template by a familiar name, without changing what the customer receives
- Syncing with Meta does not erase a nickname that was already saved
