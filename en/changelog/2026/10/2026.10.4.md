## New Features

### Edit and delete messages in internal chat

In the team’s internal chat, the author can fix a message’s text or delete it for everyone in the conversation.

#### What is it for?
- Fix a typo without sending another message
- Remove a message sent by mistake or with outdated information

#### How to use it
1. Open the internal chat (direct or group)
2. In your message menu, choose **Edit** to change the text. Everyone sees that the message was edited
3. Choose **Delete** to remove the content. Participants see that the message was deleted
4. You can only edit or delete messages you sent. Editing applies to text; audio, files, and system notices cannot be edited

> Guide: [Chat — Internal chat](/en/guide/chat/interface#internal-chat-communication-between-agents)

---

## Benefits

- Team conversations stay cleaner, without extra messages just to correct a mistake
- Everyone in the chat sees the correction or the deletion right away
