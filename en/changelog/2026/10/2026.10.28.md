## New Features

### Groups on quick filters

Inbox shortcuts can sit in **one-level groups**. The group only opens the menu; the item inside applies the cut. Drag a filter onto another’s **Group** zone to create the submenu.

#### What is it for?
- Keep similar shortcuts together (channels, queues, teams) without crowding the bar
- Show one icon and pick the cut from the dropdown
- On mobile, in icon-only mode, see the current filter with its name and scroll the rest when they don’t fit

#### How to use it?
1. In **Chats**, open **Configure quick filters** (owner or admin)
2. Drag a shortcut onto **Group** on another — both become children of a new group, using the target’s name, icon, and color
3. To reorder only, drop on the card, not on the Group zone
4. Save — in the list, the group opens the menu; the inner filter is what cuts the conversations
5. You can **pin** the group in the list layout, like the other shortcuts

The group does not filter by itself. Filters already inside a group have no Group zone (one level only). If the selected item has no counter, the chip **does not show** the siblings’ total.

More icons (email, WhatsApp, Instagram, and others in the same interface style) and hidden shortcuts appear faded in the configuration list.

> Full guide: [Conversation filters — Groups on quick filters](/en/guide/chat/filters#groups-on-quick-filters)

## Benefits

- The shortcut bar holds more cuts without becoming an endless row
- The team picks the right filter in the submenu, without applying a generic “group” cut
- On mobile, the current shortcut stays visible even with many icons
