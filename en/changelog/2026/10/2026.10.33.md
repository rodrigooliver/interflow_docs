## New Features

### Export a conversation

Each conversation menu can download the full history as PDF, TXT, CSV, or JSON. The file lists messages in order, with date, sender, and text, so the thread can be stored or shared outside the platform.

> Full guide: [Chat — Export a conversation](/en/guide/chat/interface#export-a-conversation)

#### What is it for?
- Keep a copy of the conversation for documentation or compliance
- Share the history without relying on access to the platform

#### How to use it
1. Open the conversation
2. In the conversation menu, choose **Export conversation**
3. Select **PDF**, **TXT**, **CSV**, or **JSON**
4. The file is built from the full history and the download starts right away

---

## Benefits

- The history is available as an external file
- PDF, TXT, CSV, and JSON cover reading, records, spreadsheets, and integration
