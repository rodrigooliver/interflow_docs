## New Features

### Conditional signature on the AI Agent

The agent can use a different signature depending on the channel, funnel stage, or customer tags. The signature still stays out of the text the AI reads.

#### What it is for
- Sign with one name on one channel and another name on another
- Change the signature when the customer is on a stage or has a tag
- Keep a default signature for anyone who matches no rule

#### How to use
1. Open the AI Agent on the **Sending** tab
2. Turn on **Use signature**
3. Under **Signatures by condition**, add a rule
4. Choose channel, stage, and tags, the text, and whether it appears before or after the message
5. The first matching rule is the one that goes to the chat. With no match, the default signature is used
6. The preview beside the form shows the rule you are editing

If the default signature is empty and a rule already exists, the default block stays hidden. You can open it again with **Set default signature**.

The same choice applies when the reply is sent as audio.

> Full guide: [AI Agents — Signature](/en/guide/ai-agents/#signature-in-messages)

## Benefits

- Each channel can send the right signature, without one agent per number
- The AI does not copy the signature text into later replies
