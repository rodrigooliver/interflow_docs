## New Features

### Fixed or variable behavior on a category

Each income or expense category can be marked **fixed** or **variable**. Obligations and posted entries do not get a new field: the period total uses the category on the line.

#### What it is for
- See how much of the period is a cost that does not change with volume (rent, software) and how much follows the operation (commission, freight, a one-off purchase)
- Calculate contribution margin: period income minus variable expenses
- Filter the report by cost center to look at one area

#### How to use it
1. In **Financial → Categories**, open or create a category
2. Under **Behavior**, choose **Fixed**, **Variable**, or leave **Unclassified**
3. In **Financial → Reports**, on the **By category** tab, see fixed costs, variable costs, and the margin
4. Optionally filter by cost center in the report toolbar

A cost with a fixed part and a variable part (energy with a demand charge and usage) becomes two categories. An unclassified category stays on the list and stays out of the three totals. Changing the behavior reclassifies that category's history.

## Improvements

### Category form scrolls

The create and edit category dialog stays within the screen height. The title stays put and the fields scroll when they do not fit.

## Benefits

- Managers split structure cost from cost that grows with sales without posting the amount twice
- The margin is the sum of the categories, for what is already paid and what is still open
