## New Features

### AI Decider in the flow

The flow builder gets the **AI Decider** node. The AI reads a context and picks one of the outputs you described, or none.

Usage is charged to the organization's AI credit balance.

#### What is it for?
- Route the conversation by topic without a condition for every word
- Split intents (purchase, support, complaint) into different outputs
- Take another path when the AI is not confident enough

#### How to use it?
1. From the integrations palette, drag **AI Decider**
2. Click an output title to open the panel
3. In the context, describe what the AI should read. You can insert variables, such as the last 10 or 50 messages
4. For each option, write the title (shown on the node) and the description (what the AI compares)
5. Set the minimum confidence. Below that percent, the flow follows **None**
6. Connect each output, **None**, and **Error** to the next step

If there is no credit balance, the flow follows **Error**.
