## New Features

### Channel, filters, and sale price on customers by ad

In **UTM → Customers by ad**, you can see which channel the person arrived on, filter the list, and check each customer's sale price.

#### What it is for
- Split people from the same ad who came in on different WhatsApp channels
- Narrow the report by stage, tag, or channel without mixing the rest of the base
- Export the list with channel and sale price
- See the price on the ad detail screen, next to contact, tags, and date

#### How to use
1. Open **UTM** → **Customers by ad**
2. Use **Filter by stage**, **Filter by tag**, or **Filter by channel** — filters combine
3. Open an ad: group by **Funnel**, **Tag**, or **Channel**
4. In the table, see the arrival **Channel** and the **Value** column
5. Export the CSV when you need the full spreadsheet

The channel is that customer's first individual chat (the number/channel where the conversation started).

> Full guide: [UTM — Customers by ad](/en/guide/utm/#customers-by-ad)

## Bug Fixes

### From/To period in local time

The ads report date filter used UTC and cut off the end of the last day in Brazil. **From** and **To** now cover the calendar day in the browser time zone: 10/01 includes until 23:59 on your clock.
