## New Features

### Notes and a charge in legal proceedings

When creating or editing a billing contract, you can write an internal note and mark whether the charge is in legal proceedings. The contract description stays separate.

#### What is it for?
- Record case details without mixing them into the charge description
- See which charges are in court
- Spot the **Legal** badge on the contract list

#### How to use it
1. Open **Billing** and create a contract, or open an existing one
2. Fill in **Notes**
3. Choose **Not in legal proceedings** or **In legal proceedings**
4. On the list, a legal charge shows the **Legal** badge
5. On the detail page, the pencil next to the note changes the text and the selector

On renegotiation, the note and the legal flag carry over to the new contract.

---

## Benefits

- Legal follow-up lives on the contract itself
- The team can tell from the list which charges are in court
