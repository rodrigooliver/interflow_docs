## New Features

### Assign a stage in bulk

Owners and admins can place several customers who have no stage into the funnel at once, from the customer list.

#### What it is for
- Organize people who just signed up and are not in any stage yet
- Avoid opening each profile for the same first classification

#### How to use
1. In **Customers**, select two or more customers
2. **Assign stage** only appears if at least one of them has no stage
3. Choose the funnel and the stage, then confirm

Customers who already have a stage stay as they are. After confirmation, the screen shows how many were selected and how many received the stage.

This is an administrator correction: it does not trigger a flow, pixel, or message, and it does not record history. Changing one customer's stage still works as before.

> Guide: [Customers — Assign a stage in bulk](/en/guide/crm/customers#assign-stage-in-bulk)

---

## Benefits

- Less repetitive work to classify new customers in the funnel
- Anyone who already has a stage stays put
- The result is clear: selected and updated
