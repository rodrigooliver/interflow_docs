Release date: **October 14, 2026**

## Improvements

### A tag blocks automatic deletion

When automatic customer deletion runs, **Skip customers with tags** leaves out anyone with any tag. Off, a tag does not block deletion.

Deleting customers by hand from the list works the same as before.

---

## Benefits

- Tagged contacts stay when automatic deletion runs
- The protection applies to any tag and only when the option is on
