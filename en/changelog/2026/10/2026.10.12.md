## New Features

### Dictate (turn into text)

In the chat message field, next to send, the voice-wave button turns speech into text as you talk. The audio recorder stays in place: dictation writes in the field and does not send an audio file.

The same action is in the copilot, next to send, with a microphone icon.

The organization needs an active **ElevenLabs** integration and AI credits. Usage is billed as transcription at US$ 0.39 per hour of audio. Without balance, dictation does not start.

#### How to use it

1. In chat, click the wave button next to send. In the copilot, click the microphone.
2. Speak. The text appears in the field.
3. Click stop to finish.
4. Review the text and send it as a normal message.

> Guide: [Chat — Dictate](/en/guide/chat/interface#dictate-turn-into-text) · [Copilot](/en/guide/copilot#dictate)

## Benefits

- The message is ready without typing
- Recorded audio for the customer stays separate from dictation
- Speech from before the connection is kept
- The cost shows in AI credits, like other transcriptions
