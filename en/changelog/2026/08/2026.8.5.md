## New Features

### Online / Away status for agents

Agents can mark themselves as **Away** (e.g. lunch break) from the sidebar, next to their avatar. While away:

- they leave the **rotation** for new chats in their teams
- pending reservations assigned to them are released for redistribution
- optionally, customers in chats **assigned to them** receive an automatic message when they write

The away message is customizable, and sending it is **optional** (checkbox). There is a 30-minute cooldown per conversation so the same notice is not repeated.

> Full guide: [Online / Away status](/en/guide/chat/agent-away)

#### What is it for?

- Tell the customer clearly when the agent steps away briefly
- Stop receiving new chats in the rotation during the break
- Resume normally by going Online again

#### How to use?

1. In the sidebar, next to your avatar, click **Online** (or **Away**)
2. In the modal, optionally enable **send automatic message** and edit the text
3. Confirm **Go away**
4. To return, open the status again and choose **Go online**
5. Use **Refresh** in the modal to sync status from the server

## Benefits

- Clear communication with customers during short breaks
- Team rotation respects who is away
- Simple control without leaving the chat screen
