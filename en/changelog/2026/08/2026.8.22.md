## New Features

### One-off message on a pending chat

On **pending** conversations, agents only saw **Attend**. With the option on, a second button appears — **Send a message** — so they can reply without taking the ticket.

The input does not open on its own: only after that click. If they close the conversation or open another one, the state is gone and both buttons come back. The chat stays pending; sending the message does **not** start attendance.

The option is off by default. Each organization decides whether to show the button.

> Full guide: [Settings — Allow message without attending](/en/guide/settings/#allow-message-without-attending)

#### What is it for?

- Send a quick note without taking the conversation out of the queue
- Avoid attending a ticket just to send one line
- Keep the conversation available for another agent

#### How to use?

1. In **Settings** → **Attendance**, turn on **Allow message without attending**
2. Save
3. Open a pending chat: besides **Attend**, **Send a message** appears
4. Click that button to unlock the message field
5. Send. The chat stays pending

::: tip Reservation and 24-hour window
If the chat is reserved for another agent, the reservation banner still replaces the buttons. On channels with a closed 24-hour window, after the click you see the existing notice — not the composer.
:::

## Benefits
