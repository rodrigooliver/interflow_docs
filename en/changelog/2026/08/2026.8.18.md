## New Features

### Cash payment receipt

When a customer pays an installment **in cash at the office**, you can record the payment on the charge, settle the installment, post it to finance, and **generate the receipt immediately** — print it or download a PDF.

The document follows a simple receipt layout (payer, payee, amount in numbers and words, description, date, and signature). **It does not replace a tax invoice.**

#### What is it for?

- Settle the installment as soon as the customer pays in person
- Hand over a printed or digital proof before they leave
- Keep the cash register and finance reports in sync with billing
- Reprint the receipt later if the modal is closed

#### How to use?

1. Open the **contract** in Billing (or the **overdue** list)
2. On the installment, click **Receive in cash** (or **Receive amount**, if a partial payment already exists)
3. Enter amount, date, description, and the **cash register**
4. Confirm — the installment is settled and the receipt opens right away
5. Use **Print** (two copies: customer and file) or **Download PDF**
6. To issue it again: **Reprint receipt** on the installment or in the **Manual entries** report

::: tip What already existed
Cash settlement and the finance posting were already there. This version adds the **proof of payment** and names the action the way the team uses it every day.
:::

::: warning Receipt, not invoice
This is an internal payment receipt. Businesses that need a tax invoice still issue it through their usual fiscal channel.
:::

## Benefits

- The customer leaves with a receipt, without using another website
- Installment, cash register, and report update in the same step
- Reprint is available on the contract and in the manual-entry history
- Receipt text includes the amount in words, tax ID when registered, and two copies
