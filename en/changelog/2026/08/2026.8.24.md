## New Features

### System voices and audio from text

Save organization **voices** (OpenAI, ElevenLabs, or Minimax) and, in the conversation, turn the typed text into audio — without recording on the microphone.

The **Record from text** button sits on the input bar. With **one** active voice, it generates immediately; with **two or more**, the attendant picks a voice. The script stays in the field (collapsed) so they can edit or regenerate. On send, the chat stores **one** message: the audio and the text as the transcription.

> Full guide: [System voices](/en/guide/chat/system-voices)

#### What is it for?

- Send audio in the brand voice without recording every message
- Keep tone and speed consistent across attendants
- Keep the script visible in Interflow as a transcription

#### How to use?

1. In **Settings**, add a TTS integration (OpenAI, ElevenLabs, or Minimax)
2. Open **Voices**, create the voice (name, integration, and settings), and leave it **active**
3. In the chat, type the text and click **Record from text**
4. Preview, edit or regenerate if needed, then send

::: tip Who configures it
Admin and owner create, edit, and delete voices. Any member can use **active** voices in conversations, on channels that accept audio.
:::
