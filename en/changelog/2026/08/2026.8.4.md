## New Features

### Customer macros

Automation buttons on the customer profile: one click can change kind, fill fields, assign seller/support/indication, create a task, or start a silent flow.

Each macro has visibility conditions (kind, funnel, stage, tag, or field) and can **ask on click** — a wizard collects missing data before running.

> Full guide: [Customer macros](/en/guide/crm/customer-macros)

#### What is it for?

- Standardize closing a deal (lead → client + task + flow)
- Fill required fields without opening several screens
- Assign seller, support, or indication with one button

#### How to use?

1. Open **Customer Macros** in the menu
2. Create the macro: button label and color, when to show it, what to ask, and the actions
3. In **edit customer**, buttons appear at the top of the **General** tab and also in the header actions menu (⋮)
4. Click the button — if there are prompts, complete them and run

---

### Customer kind: lead or client

The profile now has a **kind**: **Lead (sale)** or **Client (closed)**. You can filter the list and use kind in macro conditions and actions.

#### What is it for?

- Separate people still in the sales pipeline from closed clients
- Show macros only for leads (or only for clients)
