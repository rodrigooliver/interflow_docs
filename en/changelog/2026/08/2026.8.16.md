## New Features

### Bulk team adjustment

In **bulk actions** on the chat list, you can select several conversations and change only the **responsible team** at once.

This is a queue-organization adjustment — not the official transfer. The conversation does **not** get a notification, system message, or auto-assign. The agent and status (open, in progress, closed) **stay as they are**.

> Full guide: [Chat — Interface](/en/guide/chat/interface#bulk-actions)

#### What is it for?

- Move a batch of conversations to another team without opening them one by one
- Fix the wrong team on several chats at once
- Keep the attendance as it is (who is assigned and whether it is open or closed)

#### How to use?

1. In **Chats**, turn on **Bulk actions**
2. Select conversations (or **Select all filtered**)
3. In the list footer, open **Actions** → **Change team**
4. Choose the destination team and confirm the warning
5. Chats already on that team are skipped

::: tip What does not change
No push, no system message, the agent stays assigned, and a closed chat stays closed. For a real transfer (queue, notification, and team flow), use **Transfer team** on each conversation.
:::

## Benefits

- Redistribute the queue across teams in a few clicks
- Adjust without running the full transfer flow
- Compact action bar in the list footer, without covering the messages
