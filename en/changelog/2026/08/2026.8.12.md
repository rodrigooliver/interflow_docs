## New Features

### Empty or move customers from a stage

From the stage action menu, owners, admins, and managers can **clear a column in one step**: remove every customer from the funnel or move them to another stage — including a stage in another funnel.

> Full guide: [CRM — Sales funnels](/en/guide/crm/funnels#empty-or-move-customers-from-a-stage)

#### What is it for?

- Clear a crowded column without dragging cards one by one
- Close an old process (customers leave the funnel)
- Redistribute the whole batch to another stage or funnel

#### How to use?

1. Open the funnel in **CRM → Funnels**
2. On the stage, click the three dots and choose **Move or remove customers**
3. Select **Remove from funnel** or **Move to another stage**
4. If moving, pick the destination funnel and stage
5. Confirm — the action applies to **all** customers in that stage and cannot be undone

::: warning Who can use it
The option is available only to **owner**, **admin**, and **manager**. Emptying a stage **does not** start stage-change automations.
:::

---

## Benefits

- Fast column cleanup without rework
- Destination can be any funnel in the organization
- Easier to move people before deleting a stage that still has customers
