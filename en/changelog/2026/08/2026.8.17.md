## New Features

### Unread messages in custom quick filters

When configuring a **custom quick filter**, you can check **Unread only** as a criterion — and combine it with assignee, team, channel, status, and the other queue cuts.

Previously, unread existed only as a general button on the list. Now each team shortcut can store that condition.

> Full guide: [Chat — Conversation filters](/en/guide/chat/filters#unread-messages)

#### What is it for?

- Open only unread conversations for your team, channel, or funnel
- Create a shortcut per user or team, without turning on the general filter every shift
- Keep the header **Unread** button for a quick cut of the current list

#### How to use?

1. In **Chats**, open **Configure quick filters**
2. Duplicate a filter (or edit a custom one) and open **Settings**
3. Check **Unread only** and combine it with the other criteria
4. Save — when you click the shortcut, the list, chip, and count follow

::: tip What stays the same
The header **Unread** button is still available to refine the current list. Archived remains a separate toggle. Switching to a system tab turns unread off.
:::

## Benefits

- Team shortcuts with the exact combination for each context
- Fewer clicks to find what still needs a reply
- The same criterion in the panel, the shortcut, and the report
