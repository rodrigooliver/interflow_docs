## New Features

### Restrict a channel by team

When configuring a channel (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or email), admins can choose **which teams** may start conversations on that channel.

Besides **Everyone**, **By role**, and **Users**, **Who can start a conversation on this channel** now includes **By team**: select the allowed teams. Anyone who is not in one of them will not see the channel when starting a conversation with a customer.

> Full guide: [Restrict channels when starting a conversation](/en/guide/channels/start-chat-visibility)

#### What is it for?

- Split WhatsApp numbers (or other channels) across teams
- Stop one team from using another team's channel (billing, VIP, sales)
- Manage access by team instead of listing users one by one

#### How to use?

1. Open **Channels** and enter the channel
2. Under **Settings**, go to **Who can start a conversation on this channel**
3. Choose **By team** and select the teams
4. Save

Owners and admins still see every channel. The default team for the ticket (who the conversation is assigned to) is a separate setting.

---

## Benefits

- Each team only uses the channels that belong to it
- Less misuse of numbers and accounts
- Simpler administration when you run several teams
