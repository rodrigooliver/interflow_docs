## New Features

### More counters on quick filters, per user or team

Conversation shortcuts are no longer stuck at **4 numbers** for everyone. You can now turn the counter on for **up to 12** quick filters — and combine that with visibility by **team, role, or person**.

Each area sees only the shortcuts (and the numbers) that match the job. Sales follows theirs; support, theirs. The list stays clearer, and the system updates only what that person actually sees.

> Full guide: [Chat — Conversation filters](/en/guide/chat/filters#counters-on-quick-filters)
>
> Blog article: [How to track the queue with numbers per team](/en/blog/conversation-filter-counters-per-team)

#### What is it for?

- Watch more queues at once (unread, unassigned, my team, a channel, a funnel)
- Build a set of numbers per team or person, without one company-wide pattern
- Keep the screen tidy: people who don’t need that shortcut don’t see the counter either

#### How to use?

1. In **Chats**, open **Configure quick filters**
2. Create or duplicate shortcuts (criteria, name, icon, and color)
3. On each shortcut, set **who can see it**: everyone, teams, roles, or users
4. Click the **counter** icon on the filters that should show a number (up to 12 for the organization)
5. Save — each person sees only the shortcuts released to them, with the numbers beside them

::: tip Who configures it
Admin and owner build the shortcuts and turn counters on. Agents only see in the bar what was released to their role, team, or user.
:::

## Benefits

- A queue adapted to each area, without forcing the same 4 numbers on everyone
- More metrics in view, without cluttering the screen of people who don’t use that cut
- More effective management: everyone follows what matters for their job
