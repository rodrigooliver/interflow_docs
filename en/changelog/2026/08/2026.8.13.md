## New Features

### Notifications per WhatsApp group

Each user can **turn notifications on or off** for a WhatsApp group — only for themselves. The default stays on. Busy groups stop interrupting work without disappearing from the list.

> Full guide: [Chat — WhatsApp group notifications](/en/guide/chat/whatsapp-group-notifications)

#### What is it for?

- Mute noisy groups without losing the history
- Keep seeing messages and the unread badge
- Get alerts only for the groups that matter

#### How to use?

1. Open the WhatsApp group in **Chats**
2. Click the group name to open **Edit Group**
3. At the top, use the **Notifications** switch
4. Or, in the list, open the ⋮ menu and choose **Mute notifications** / **Unmute notifications**

::: tip What changes
Turning it off stops **push** and **sound**. The conversation, the list, and unread counts **stay**. The choice is yours only — teammates still get the alert.
:::

## Benefits

- Less distraction in high-volume groups
- Personal control, without changing the group for the team
- Available in the group modal, the header, and the list
