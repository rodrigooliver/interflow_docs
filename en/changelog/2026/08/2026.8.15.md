## New Features

### Reopen the chat if the customer replies after closing

After an agent closes a conversation, a “thanks”, “ok”, or any other customer message used to open a **new ticket**. With the window enabled, that message returns on the **same ticket**, to the previous agent or the team queue.

The option is off by default. Each organization sets how many minutes apply after closing (1 to 1440).

> Full guide: [Settings — Reopen after closing](/en/guide/settings/#reopen-the-chat-after-closing)

#### What is it for?

- Avoid a new conversation just because the customer thanked or confirmed
- Keep the history on the same ticket number
- Return the chat to the previous agent, when there was one, or to the team queue

#### How to use?

1. In **Settings** → **Attendance**, turn on **Reopen chat if the customer replies after closing**
2. Set the window in minutes (15 when you first enable it)
3. Save
4. After closing a chat, if the customer replies within that window, the same ticket reopens

::: tip What is included
Only **customer** messages on individual chats. Groups and messages sent from the agent’s phone do not reopen the ticket. Outside the window, behavior stays the same: a new conversation.
:::

---

## Benefits

- Fewer redundant tickets right after closing
- Continuous history on the same conversation
- Per-organization control, with no change for teams that leave it off
