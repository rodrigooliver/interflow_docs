## New Features

### Filter conversations by customer custom fields

The chat list, attendance report, and quick filters now accept the **same customer custom fields** you already use in Customers: select, multi-select, date, and date/time.

The queue shows only conversations that match the profile you care about — plan, source, due date — without opening each record.

> Full guide: [Chat — Conversation filters](/en/guide/chat/filters)

#### What is it for?

- Find conversations by a value on the customer (e.g. plan, source)
- See who still **has no value** on a field
- Narrow the queue by a date or date/time range
- Reuse the same cut in the report and in team quick filters

#### How to use?

1. In **Chats**, open the filters panel
2. In **Custom fields**, choose the mode: all, blank, or specific values/period
3. Apply — the list and counts follow
4. In the report, use the same criteria before exporting
5. Admins: **Configure quick filters** → add the criteria to the team shortcut

::: tip What is included
The filter looks at the **customer record** linked to the conversation. Groups and chats without a customer are left out. Text, number, and yes/no fields do not filter the list yet — but they are available as **columns** in the report.
:::

### Custom field columns in the report

In the attendance report export, the **Columns to export** list includes **all** customer custom fields. Check the ones that should appear in the file.

#### How to use?

1. In **Chats**, open **Export report**
2. On the right, scroll to **Custom fields**
3. Check the fields you want in the file
4. Export — each field becomes a column, with the customer value from the conversation

Groups and chats without a customer get an empty cell. Fields start unchecked, so the current report does not change until you pick them.

## Bug Fixes

### WhatsApp funnel conversion without a registered ad

Moving a customer to a stage with a Meta conversion event used to require a registered ad or pixel in UTM when the origin was WhatsApp (click-to-WhatsApp / CTWA). That is not needed: only the connected **official WhatsApp channel** and the customer's **click identifier** (`ctwa_clid`) are required.

The event now reaches Meta in that case even without a registered ad. Other origins (website, form, account pixel) still need an ad or pixel as before.
