## New Features

### Teams when inviting a user

When inviting a new user, you can **select service teams in the same invite step**. Default teams are suggested (pre-selected), and membership is applied as soon as the invite is sent.

#### What is it for?

- Avoid opening another menu just to add the user to the right teams
- Reduce misconfiguration when onboarding agents
- Make signup faster for admins who are less familiar with the platform

#### How to use?

1. Open **Organization → Users** (or **Members**)
2. Click **Send Invitation** / **Invite**
3. Fill in name, email, and role
4. Under **Teams**, select the desired teams (suggested ones are already checked)
5. Send the invite — the user is added to the chosen teams as a **member**

> To make someone a **team leader**, keep using **Organization → Teams** after the invite.

---

## Benefits

- Fewer clicks and less switching between menus
- Lower chance of inviting someone and forgetting to add them to teams
- More intuitive user onboarding flow
