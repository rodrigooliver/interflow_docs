## New Features

### Protection against WhatsApp new-contact blocks (WAHA)

WhatsApp can restrict **first contact** (a number that channel has not talked to yet). The session stays connected. Interflow now detects that restriction, shows it on screen, and **stops only cold outreach** — existing conversations keep working.

> Guide: [WhatsApp WAHA — New-contact restriction](/en/guide/channels/whatsapp-waha#new-contact-restriction)
>
> Campaigns: [Bulk messages](/en/guide/chat/bulk-messages#whatsapp-waha)

#### What is it for?

- Avoid making the block worse with more first contacts
- Keep the team serving people who already talked, even while the restriction is active
- Pause a WAHA campaign immediately instead of retrying
- Count first contacts in the day when several people share the same number (optional cap)

#### How to use?

1. On the WAHA channel card, **Connected** stays green. If WhatsApp restricts new numbers, an amber badge appears
2. Open the channel: the banner shows until when the restriction applies and asks you **not to restart or disconnect** the session
3. In **Bulk messages** with a WAHA channel and message mode, leave **Send only to existing conversations** checked (default)
4. If several people share the same number, in **Channels** → WAHA channel you can set an **extra daily limit** (0 turns it off; WhatsApp still sets the real cap)

When the restriction ends, the campaign **does not** resume by itself — you need to start it again.

## Benefits

- Lower risk of making a WhatsApp block worse
- Warm conversations keep flowing during the restriction
- Campaigns stop instead of burning the queue
- Optional daily count when the team cannot track volume
