## New Features

### Move appointments between schedules

When editing an appointment, you can change the **Schedule** without deleting and recreating the booking. Customer, history, chat, and other links stay on the same record.

Works for **one-off** and **recurring** appointments. For recurring ones, the usual edit scope still applies:

| Scope | What moves to the new schedule |
|-------|--------------------------------|
| **This only** | Only the selected occurrence |
| **This and future** | This and future ones (the series will keep generating on the new schedule) |
| **All** | The entire series |

#### What is it for?

- Fix appointments created on the wrong schedule
- Redistribute bookings across schedules without rework
- Keep history, reminders, and links intact

#### How to use?

1. Open the appointment in **Schedule**
2. In the **Schedule** field, choose the destination schedule
3. Select **Service** and **Provider** again from the new schedule (they are cleared when you switch)
4. Save
5. If it is recurring, choose the scope: this only, this and future, or all

::: tip Service and provider
Each schedule has its own services and providers. After changing schedule, pick the correct values for the destination before saving.
:::
