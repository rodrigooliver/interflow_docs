## New Features

### Auxiliary channel as the default

On official WhatsApp with a connected auxiliary channel, replies go out on the auxiliary channel even while the 24-hour window is open. This covers attendants, the AI, flow messages, and scheduled messages. The option lives on the official channel and starts off. Templates stay on the official channel.

#### What is it for?
- Agents no longer switch the channel by hand on every conversation
- Fewer replies go out on the official channel when the team agreed to use the auxiliary one
- The auxiliary channel stays selected for the next messages in that conversation
- The same default applies when the AI replies, a flow sends text, or a scheduled message goes out

#### How to use it?
1. Open the **official WhatsApp** channel and go to **WAHA auxiliary channel**
2. Turn on **Use the auxiliary channel by default**
3. Opening a conversation for that number starts sending on the auxiliary channel
4. Switch to **Official** only for that conversation if needed; the next one follows the default again

> Full guide: [WhatsApp WAHA — Auxiliary channel](/en/guide/channels/whatsapp-waha#auxiliary-channel-for-whatsapp-official)
