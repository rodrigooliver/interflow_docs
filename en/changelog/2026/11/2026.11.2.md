## New Features

### Person being served on the appointment

A booking can record who will attend, separate from the customer talking on WhatsApp. It covers someone booking on behalf of another person, such as a parent booking for a child.

#### What is it for?
- Keep the conversation on the responsible customer and the attendee's name on the booking
- Let the same contact hold overlapping times for different people
- Ask for that name in the Agent IA scheduling tool, only on the schedule where the option is on

#### How to use it?
1. Open the schedule and, under rules, turn on **Identify the person being served**
2. When creating or editing the booking, fill in **Person being served**
3. In Agent IA, the tool for the selected schedule starts accepting that name

> Full guide: [Schedule — Person being served](/en/guide/schedule/#person-being-served)

---

## Benefits

- The team sees the contact and the person who will attend on the calendar
- Dependents and family members do not need another customer record
- The AI only receives the field when the schedule chosen in the action is configured
