## New Features

### Ratings of closed chats

In Reports, the **Ratings of closed chats** card lists conversations closed in the period and the 1-to-5 score when the customer rated them. Without a rating, the score stays blank. The comment appears on the same row.

#### What it is for
- See how many conversations closed with a score and how many did not
- Compare the average and the spread from 1 to 5
- Read the comment on each conversation
- Filter by team, agent, and whether there was a rating
- Take the list to a spreadsheet

#### How to use it
1. Open **Reports**
2. Choose **Ratings of closed chats**
3. Select today, 7 days, 30 days, or a custom range
4. Filter by team, agent, and by all, rated only, or unrated only
5. Export CSV if you need the list outside the screen

Internal groups are left out. The score still comes from the rating flow, in the chat score and comment fields.

> Full guide: [Reports — Ratings of closed chats](/en/guide/reports/#ratings-of-closed-chats)

## Benefits

- The score is no longer only inside each conversation
- You can see rating coverage, not only the average of people who answered
- The comment comes with ticket, customer, channel, team, and agent
