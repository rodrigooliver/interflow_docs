## New Features

### Bus seat map

An organization with the **Buses** module can draw the vehicle, open trips, and hold a seat during service. The hold marks the seat as taken and can be linked to a customer and one of that customer's chats. There is no payment and no ticket.

#### What it is for
- Build a bus with one or two decks, doors, stairs, a bathroom, a water fountain, the driver, and emergency exits on the outer sides
- Choose one or two columns on each side
- Open a trip with a time and a duration, without using the same bus on overlapping times
- Reserve and release a seat on the trip page
- Let the AI attendant list trips, read the map, and reserve, when the **Bus seats** action is on the prompt

#### How to use it
1. On the organization list, turn on the **Buses** module
2. Under **Appointments**, open **Buses**
3. Register the vehicle and draw it on its own page
4. Create the trip and open the map to reserve
5. On the agent, add the **Bus seats** action

Without the module on the organization, the menu stays hidden and the API refuses the request.

## Benefits

- The trip map follows the bus drawing
- A reserved seat stays linked to the customer and, if you choose, to a chat
- The same vehicle does not run two trips in the same window
