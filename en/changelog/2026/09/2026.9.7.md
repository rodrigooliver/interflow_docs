## New Features

### Cost centers in Financial

The **Financial** module now lets you create cost centers and assign them to expenses. This new dimension shows how much each department, unit, or operation consumes without changing category organization.

#### What is it for?

- Separate expenses by department, unit, project, or operation
- Filter entries by cost center
- Compare settled, open, and total amounts
- Preserve history when a cost center is deactivated

#### How to use it

1. Open **Financial → Cost centers**
2. Enter a code, name, and optional description
3. Select the cost center when creating or editing an expense
4. Use the filter in **Entries** to find that center's expenses
5. In **Reports**, open **By cost center** and export the CSV when needed

Cost centers are optional and only available for expenses. Existing entries remain available under **Without cost center**.

> Full guide: [Financial — cost centers](/en/guide/financial/#cost-centers)

## Benefits

- Clearer visibility into where company resources are consumed
