## New Features

### Locations and rooms on the schedule

Each schedule can have **locations** — rooms, booths, or units with a free name. When the option is on, the location becomes the control center: it has its own hours, allowed services and professionals, and the appointment must pick a room.

You can block two appointments in the same location at the same time, even with different services or professionals. In the list, the appointment card shows the location; filters and the column split also get **By location**.

#### What is it for?

- A clinic or salon with several rooms at the same time
- A unit with booths that cannot overlap
- See occupancy by room, not only by professional
- Let the AI Agent book only in specific locations

#### How to use it?

1. Open the schedule → **Edit** → turn on **Use locations**
2. Optionally turn on **Block duplicate location (this schedule)**
3. Under **Professionals/Services**, add the locations (name, color, services, and professionals)
4. Under **Availability**, set the hours for each location
5. When creating an appointment, choose the **Location** — the field is required on that schedule
6. In the appointment list, filter by location or split columns **By location**
7. In **Schedule** → **Schedules**, the card shows how many locations that schedule has

On the AI Agent, schedule actions now respect locations: all of them, or only the ones you select.

> Full guide: [Schedule — Locations and rooms](/en/guide/schedule/locations)

### Duplicate a schedule

You clone a configured schedule and get a ready-to-use copy under a new name. The new schedule inherits services, professionals, locations, hours, holidays, and notifications. Appointments are **not** copied — the copy starts empty.

Organization flows stay the same: the copy reuses the existing links (status and templates) and does not create new flows.

#### What is it for?

- Open a new unit, room, or team without re-entering the setup
- Use a schedule as a template and change only what differs
- Try a variation without touching the live schedule

#### How to use it?

1. Open **Schedule** → **Schedules**
2. On the card, click **Duplicate schedule** — or, inside the schedule, use the same button next to edit and delete
3. Confirm or change the name (the default is `{name} (copy)`)
4. The copy opens on the management screen, ready to adjust

What is copied: schedule rules, services, professionals, locations, availability, holidays, and notification templates.

What is left out: appointment history, reminders already sent, recurring series, and AI agent actions that point only to the original schedule.

> Full guide: [Schedule — Duplicate a schedule](/en/guide/schedule/#duplicate-a-schedule)
