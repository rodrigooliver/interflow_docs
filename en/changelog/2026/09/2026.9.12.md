## New Features

### Duplicate a schedule

You clone a configured schedule and get a ready-to-use copy under a new name. The new schedule inherits services, professionals, locations, hours, holidays, and notifications. Appointments are **not** copied — the copy starts empty.

Organization flows stay the same: the copy reuses the existing links (status and templates) and does not create new flows.

#### What is it for?

- Open a new unit, room, or team without re-entering the setup
- Use a schedule as a template and change only what differs
- Try a variation without touching the live schedule

#### How to use it?

1. Open **Schedule** → **Schedules**
2. On the card, click **Duplicate schedule** — or, inside the schedule, use the same button next to edit and delete
3. Confirm or change the name (the default is `{name} (copy)`)
4. The copy opens on the management screen, ready to adjust

What is copied: schedule rules, services, professionals, locations, availability, holidays, and notification templates.

What is left out: appointment history, reminders already sent, recurring series, and AI agent actions that point only to the original schedule.

> Full guide: [Schedule — Duplicate a schedule](/en/guide/schedule/#duplicate-a-schedule)

### Location count on the list card

When a schedule uses locations, the list card shows how many locations are registered, next to professionals and services.

#### What is it for?

- See at a glance whether the schedule has rooms or spaces set up
- Tell location-based schedules apart from those that only use professionals

#### How to use it?

1. Open **Schedule** → **Schedules**
2. If the schedule has locations enabled, the card shows a third count, **Locations**

> Full guide: [Schedule — Locations on the list card](/en/guide/schedule/#locations-on-the-list-card)
