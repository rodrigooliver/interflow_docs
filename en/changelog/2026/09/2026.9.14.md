## New Features

### Copilot

The copilot is an assistant inside the system, for the person operating Interflow. It answers how to use the product, reads the open screen, and looks up conversations, tasks, the schedule, the funnel, and the customer with the same access as your account.

There are three modes: **Ask** (read only), **Agent** (changes after you confirm), and **Direct agent** (changes immediately). In ask mode it does not save or send.

You can choose the model, the reasoning effort, and whether the call uses the Interflow credit balance or an organization integration. Passwords are not filled in.

#### What is it for?

- Ask how something works and get the documentation link
- Find a customer and open the conversation without searching the list
- Request a task, an appointment, a tag, or a stage change without leaving the screen
- See what is on the current page and point at the right button

#### How to use it?

1. Click **Copilot**
2. Choose **Ask**, **Agent**, or **Direct agent**
3. Write the request. On an attendance, the conversation is already attached
