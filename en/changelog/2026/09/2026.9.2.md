## New Features

### Menu by module

The sidebar is now organized by **module** (Inbox, Finance, POS, Billing, HR / Time clock, and Medical records). Switch modules at the top of the bar, under the organization. Each module shows only what that workspace needs.

CRM stays in **Inbox**. Customers, appointments, and requests can appear in more than one module without switching the workspace on their own.

#### What is it for?

- Find Finance, POS, or Billing without scrolling one long menu
- Keep daily work (chats, sales) apart from setup
- Stay in the module you chose when opening shared screens, such as all customers

#### How to use?

1. At the top of the sidebar, open the module switcher
2. Pick the workspace (for example, **Inbox** or **Finance**)
3. Use the **Automation**, **Reports**, and **Settings** headings to find the right group
4. **Settings** in the footer opens account, team, and subscription
5. In Inbox, the **Settings** block groups inbox preferences, channels, tags, macros, users, and teams

> Guide: [Settings — Menu and search](/en/guide/settings/#menu-and-search)

### Menu search (⌘K / Ctrl+K)

Search modules, pages, and settings without memorizing the path. The shortcut is **⌘K** on Mac and **Ctrl+K** on Windows or Linux. There is also a magnifier at the top of the sidebar.

Search follows the interface language. In CRM and Customers, if you can see everyone, you get **mine** and **all**. You can also find items inside pages — for example, optional agent signature or billing history — and jump straight to that block.

#### What is it for?

- Find Subscription, Tags, or Preferences without walking the whole menu
- Open CRM filtered (mine or all) in one step
- Find a toggle inside Inbox preferences

#### How to use?

1. Click the sidebar magnifier or press **⌘K** / **Ctrl+K**
2. Type the name (e.g. `crm`, `subscription`, `whatsapp`)
3. Use the arrows and **Enter**, or click the result
4. **Esc** closes search

> Guide: [Settings — Menu and search](/en/guide/settings/#menu-and-search)
