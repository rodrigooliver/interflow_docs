## New features

### Audio replies on the AI Agent

On the **Advanced** tab, with **Send messages from the agent** on, the prompt can reply with audio using a [system voice](/en/guide/chat/system-voices) (ElevenLabs, Minimax, or OpenAI). You do not need TTS nodes in the flow.

You can choose:

- If the customer sent audio, always reply with audio
- If the reply contains a link, send text
- The chance of audio when the customer sent text (e.g. 25% is 1 in 4)

The text is rewritten for speech; the audio arrives with that version as the transcription. If any step fails, the customer gets the original text.

#### How to use it

1. Register a voice in **Voices**
2. Open the AI Agent → **Advanced**
3. Turn on **Send messages from the agent** and **Reply with audio**
4. Choose the voice and the rules
5. Remove TTS / audio nodes from the flow if they still exist, to avoid duplicates

> Full guide: [AI Agents — audio replies](/en/guide/ai-agents/#audio-replies)

## Benefits

- The same audio logic as the flow, without extra nodes
- Brand voice reused from System voices
- The customer still gets a reply if TTS fails
