## New Features

### Automatic team-to-team rotation

A channel can pick which team receives each conversation — the same idea as people rotation, but across teams. The queue belongs to the channel: you choose the teams and the order. New contacts arriving on WhatsApp, Instagram, or Facebook go to the next team. When transferring or leaving a chat, you can **send it to rotation** instead of keeping it on the current team or picking a team by hand.

After the pick, the destination team’s settings apply: if it has people rotation, a member is assigned or reserved; otherwise the chat stays pending for anyone to take.

#### What is it for?

- Spread demand across teams instead of overloading the same one
- Stop a channel from sending every new contact to a single default team
- Return the chat to a fair queue when leaving or transferring, without picking a team on the spot
- Let admins decide whether agents can pick a team or only send to rotation

#### How to use it?

1. Open the channel under **Channels** and enable **Team rotation**
2. Add at least two teams and set the queue order
3. Keep the **default team** as fallback if the queue becomes invalid
4. In **Transfer** or **Leave attendance**, choose **Send to team rotation** when you want a draw
5. In **Settings** → **Attendance**, choose whether agents can pick a specific team or only send to rotation

Admins and owners can still pick a team. Manual chat creation, campaigns, and flows are not part of this queue.

## Benefits

- Fairer load across teams, without a manual decision on every chat
- A channel-level queue, while the destination team still controls people rotation
- Faster handoff when leaving or transferring without picking a team
