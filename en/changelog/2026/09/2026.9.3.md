Release date: **September 10, 2026**

---

## New Features

### 24-hour window in flows

The **Condition** node can now identify whether a WhatsApp Official conversation is inside or outside the 24-hour customer service window, based on the customer's latest message.

#### What is it for?

- Send direct messages only while the window is open
- Route conversations outside the window to an approved template
- Combine this check with channel, team, tags, and other flow conditions

### Send WhatsApp templates

The Flow Builder now includes a **WhatsApp Template** node. You can map an approved template to each WhatsApp Official channel and fill its parameters with fixed values or flow variables.

#### How to use?

1. Add a **Condition** node and choose **WhatsApp 24-hour window**
2. Use the open-window branch for direct messages
3. Add **WhatsApp Template** to the closed-window branch
4. Select an approved template for each official channel and map its variables

> Full guide: [WhatsApp templates in flows](/en/guide/flows/nodes/whatsapp-template)

---

## Benefits

- Automations aligned with WhatsApp Official customer service rules
- Fewer failed sends outside the permitted window
- One flow can use different templates depending on the channel that runs it
- Personalize templates with customer data and variables collected by the flow
