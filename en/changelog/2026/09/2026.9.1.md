## New Features

### Alert when WhatsApp WAHA stays disconnected

If a WhatsApp (WAHA) channel **was already connected** and then drops, the owner and admins get a push with the **channel name** and **phone number**. Tapping it opens the channel so you can reconnect.

The alert is **not** instant. Interflow waits a few minutes — short session restarts do not trigger it. It also **does not** fire if you disconnect or delete the instance in the form, or if QR scanning fails before the channel was serving chats.

> Guide: [WhatsApp WAHA — Reconnection](/en/guide/channels/whatsapp-waha#reconnection)

#### What is it for?

- Learn that the number dropped without staring at the channels screen
- Jump to the right channel, with the name and number on the alert
- Avoid false alarms when the session only restarts or when you disconnect it yourself

#### How to use?

1. Keep push enabled in the app or browser (organization owner and admins)
2. If a connected WAHA channel stays offline for real, the alert arrives on its own
3. Tap the notification to open the channel and scan the QR again
4. If you click **Disconnect** (deletes the instance), nobody gets a push

::: tip Who receives it
Only the organization owner and admins. Agents are not notified.
:::

## Benefits

- Less time with WhatsApp down and nobody noticing
- Alerts only when an active session actually drops
- Disconnecting in the form stays a deliberate action, with no notification
