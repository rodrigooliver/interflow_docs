## New Features

### Financial reports

**Financial → Reports** is no longer a placeholder. It shows a management view of the cash registers you operate: cash flow, categories, receivables/payables, and a per-register comparison.

#### What is it for?

- See settled income and paid expenses in the period, plus open (pending/overdue) amounts
- See where money comes from (category and payment method)
- Age receivables and payables (current through 90+ days)
- Compare only cash registers you can access — a register without access never appears in totals

Reports appear only for **cash register admins** or operators with **Can view reports**.

#### How to use it?

1. Open **Financial → Reports**
2. Pick a period and, optionally, a cash register (or **All cashiers** you can access)
3. Switch tabs: cash flow, category, receivable/payable, and by register
4. If your profile can export reports, download the current tab as CSV

> Guide: [Financial — reports](/en/guide/financial/#reports)

## Benefits

- Totals and charts stay limited to the operator's cash registers, even for organization admins
- Reports menu hidden without operator permission
- Export only with the `canExportReports` permission
- Same glass UI as the other financial screens
