## New Features

### List and day views split by professional or schedule

In **List** and **Day** views, the calendar can be split into columns by professional or by schedule. You pick which columns to show; the choice is remembered on the next visit.

In the list, times line up as a timeline: the same start time sits on the same row across columns. With several columns, cards become compact (no duplicated time or avatar). On desktop, hover opens a panel with details and actions.

#### What is it for?

- Compare each professional’s load on the same day
- See several schedules side by side without changing filters
- Confirm, start, or cancel from the compact card panel

#### How to use?

1. Open **Schedule** > **Appointments**
2. In the view menu, choose **List** or **Day**
3. Select **By professional** or **By schedule**
4. Check the columns you want (or **Select all**) and confirm

> Full guide: [Schedule — column split](/en/guide/schedule/#column-split)

### Start an appointment that is not confirmed yet

When status is **Scheduled**, actions now include **Start**, along with confirm and cancel. You can skip confirmation and go straight to in progress, from the card and from the appointment form.

## Bug Fixes

### Split columns remembered after refresh

The professionals or schedules chosen for the column split were lost on reload. They are now saved in the browser.

## Benefits

- Operational view of several professionals or schedules on one screen
- Fewer clicks to move an appointment forward
- A clearer list when many columns are open
