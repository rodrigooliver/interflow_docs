## New Features

### Transfer between cashiers

In **Posted entries**, you move balance from one cash register to another without creating income and expense by hand. The system posts immediately: it leaves the source and enters the destination. Both balances change; the P&L **does not** treat this as income or expense.

#### What is it for?

- Move money between operating cash registers
- Keep each register’s balance right without inflating results
- See the movement on the statement, with the other register shown

#### How to use?

1. Open **Finance → Posted entries**
2. Click **Transfer**
3. Choose source, destination, amount, description, and date
4. Save — both entries appear on the statement with a **Transfer** badge

The creator must be an operator with create permission on **both** registers. Editing amount, description, or date updates the pair; deleting removes both.

> Full guide: [Finance — Transfer between cashiers](/en/guide/financial/#transfer-between-cashiers)

### Push for accounts due today

Every day at 8:00 a.m. (Brasília time), cash-register operators get a push for obligations **due today**. One notice per person per register, with no repeat on the same day. Tapping opens the list already filtered to that register and today.

#### What is it for?

- Not depending on opening Finance to remember today’s due items
- Notifying only the people who operate that register
- Going straight to today’s accounts

#### How to use?

1. Be an operator on the register, with view permission (or register admin)
2. Keep app notifications on
3. On the due date, the notice arrives in the morning; tap to open the list

Transfers between cashiers do not trigger this notice.

> Full guide: [Finance — Due-today notice](/en/guide/financial/#due-today-notice)
