## Version format

Format **YYYY.MM.N** (year.month.release number in the month).

| Example | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| `2026.7.11` | 11th release of July 2026 |
| `2026.6.9` | 9th release of June 2026 |

Each version page includes the release date, what changed, and links to product guides when relevant.
