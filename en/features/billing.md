# Billing (Contracts)

Manage contracts with installments, splits between recipients, and Asaas integration — from single payment links to automatic revenue sharing between professionals.

## What it's for

- Offices and clinics with recurring contracts and installments
- Operations with fee splits between professionals or partners
- Online sales needing payment links without a pre-registered tax ID
- Finance teams tracking payouts, write-offs, and manual distribution

## What you can do

- Create contracts with installments and status: Draft, Active, Completed, or Cancelled
- Reuse contract templates to speed up new sales
- Configure recipients with percentage, fixed amount, fee, or remainder
- Run automatic splits with payout reports
- Integrate with Asaas: charge per installment or single installment payment link
- Receive payment webhooks and record manual write-offs
- Cancel installments sequentially in Asaas
- Link contracts to the customer profile in CRM

## How it connects

- **[CRM & Customers](/en/features/crm)** — customer linked to each contract
- **[Documents](/en/features/documents)** — proposals and contracts generated from templates
- **[Partner Portal](/en/features/partner)** — billing for resold organizations
- **[Financial](/en/features/financial)** — separate modules; Financial handles internal operations
