# HR / Time Tracking

Track work hours with a digital time clock, geolocation, hour bank, and management payroll — all with notifications for managers.

## What it's for

- Companies with on-site or remote work hour tracking
- HR managers who need management payroll, absences, and holiday calendar
- Employees clocking in via the app with photo and GPS
- Operations requiring geofence to validate work location

## What you can do

- Record time punches: clock in, break, return, and clock out, with photo and GPS
- Define locations with geofence and home office rules per employee
- Configure a schedule with real times (clock-in, lunch, clock-out) and a contractual load (44h by default)
- Register absences (medical leave, individual time off) with attachments
- Track the hour bank, monthly report, and management payroll preview (4 punches, lateness, OT, CSV)
- Choose where overtime goes (bank, offset, or pay) in HR settings
- Receive push notifications for managers on each time punch
- Access the employee view in **My HR Profile**, separate from the admin panel

::: info Activation
The HR module is disabled by default. Enable it via superadmin in Admin → Organizations → Modules.
:::

## How it connects

- **[Settings](/en/guide/settings/)** — module activation and permissions per organization
- Standalone module — minimal direct integration with Chat, CRM, or Schedule
