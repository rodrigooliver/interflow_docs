# Multichannel Support

Centralize WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, and Email in a single inbox, with unified history and real-time connection status.

## What it's for

- Businesses that support customers across multiple channels without losing context
- High-volume operations that need the official WhatsApp Business API
- Small and medium businesses connecting WhatsApp via QR Code (WAHA)
- Teams that need to restrict who can start conversations on sensitive channels

## What you can do

- Connect **WhatsApp Business API** (official) with Meta-approved templates, 24-hour window, and payment support
- Connect **WhatsApp WAHA** via QR Code, with group support, history sync, and use as an auxiliary channel alongside Official
- Integrate **Instagram Direct**, **Facebook Messenger**, and **Email** (IMAP/SMTP) with threading and attachments
- Monitor each channel's status: Connected, Reconnecting, or Disconnected
- Use [WhatsApp templates](/en/guide/channels/whatsapp-templates) for conversations outside the 24-hour window
- Transcribe audio automatically via [OpenAI Whisper](/en/guide/channels/audio-transcription) — useful for AI and readable history
- Control [start-chat visibility](/en/guide/channels/start-chat-visibility) by profile or user (VIP, billing, sales channels)
- Receive real-time delivery and read receipts on messages

## How it connects

- **[Chat & Support](/en/features/chat)** — all channel conversations arrive in the unified inbox
- **[Automation & AI](/en/features/automation)** — flows and agents respond automatically by channel
- **[CRM & Customers](/en/features/crm)** — customers are created automatically when a conversation starts
- **[Reports](/en/features/reports)** — comparative metrics by source channel
