# POS (Point of Sale)

Record counter sales, manage products, orders, inventory, and tables — with CRM and financial module integration.

## What it's for

- Retail with barcode scanning and fast counter sales
- Restaurants managing tables (available, occupied, reserved)
- Stores with quotes, delivery, pickup, or dine-in
- Cashiers with session-specific permissions

## What you can do

- View a dashboard with sales KPIs and performance
- Record sales with cart, product search, and barcode scanning
- Manage products with variations and inventory control
- Create orders in modes: POS, quote, delivery, pickup, or dine-in
- Control restaurant tables: available, occupied, or reserved
- Open and close POS cash register sessions with inventory movement
- Configure store, receipt, payment methods, and table layout
- Link CRM customer to the sale and trigger sales via POS node in flows

## How it connects

- **[CRM & Customers](/en/features/crm)** — customer associated with the sale; orders preserved on bulk deletion
- **[Financial](/en/features/financial)** — revenue generated from sales
- **[Automation & AI](/en/features/automation)** — POS node in automation flows
