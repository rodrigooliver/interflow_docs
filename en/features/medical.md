# Medical Module (EMR)

Electronic health records integrated with CRM: patients, appointments, clinical records, prescriptions, and certificates — with unified support and schedule history.

## What it's for

- Clinics and practices needing records tied to customer support
- Doctors (`medical_doctor`) recording appointments and prescribing medications
- Medical assistants (`medical_assistant`) supporting the clinical workflow
- Operations unifying patient records, chat, schedule, and documents

::: info Activation
Requires the `medical` module enabled for the organization.
:::

## What you can do

- Use patient as CRM customer — unified support history and data
- Record appointments and clinical records on the patient file
- Issue prescriptions and certificates from reusable clinical templates
- Access the record with tabs: appointments, clinical notes, prescriptions, certificates, and attachments
- View appointments and consultation history on the same profile
- Generate clinical documents with CRM variables and document templates

## How it connects

- **[CRM & Customers](/en/features/crm)** — patient shares the customer record
- **[Schedule](/en/features/schedule)** — appointments on the patient record
- **[Documents](/en/features/documents)** — clinical templates for prescriptions and certificates
- **[Chat & Support](/en/features/chat)** — conversation history on the unified profile
