# Document Templates

Generate contracts, proposals, forms, and custom reports from templates with dynamic variables, helpers, and conditionals — exporting to PDF or DOCX.

## What it's for

- Legal and commercial teams needing customer-specific contracts and proposals
- Finance issuing documents with amounts in words and installment details
- Clinics generating prescriptions and certificates from clinical templates
- Multilingual operations (PT/EN/ES) with content adapted by condition

## What you can do

- Create templates in the [WYSIWYG HTML editor](/en/guide/documents/templates) (Jodit) with PDF and DOCX export
- Insert [dynamic variables](/en/guide/documents/variables) from customer, system, and custom fields
- Use Handlebars helpers: text formatting, currency, dates, numbers in words, and math operations
- Apply [conditionals](/en/guide/documents/conditionals): `if`, `ifExists`, `ifEquals`, `ifGreaterThan`, and more
- Configure watermark (text or image) and PDF margins
- Send documents automatically via the document node in automation flows

## How it connects

- **[CRM & Customers](/en/features/crm)** — customer data, address, funnel, and tags feed the variables
- **[Automation & AI](/en/features/automation)** — flows generate and send documents during support
- **[Billing](/en/features/billing)** — proposals and contracts with amounts and installments in words
- **[Medical module](/en/features/medical)** — clinical templates for prescriptions and certificates
