# CRM & Customers

Manage your customer base with complete profiles, custom fields, Kanban funnels, and automations — all integrated with support and the rest of the platform.

## What it's for

- Sales teams tracking opportunities in visual funnels
- Agents who need full customer context during support
- Administrators importing databases, merging duplicates, and controlling access by team
- Operations segmenting customers by tags and source for campaigns and bulk messaging

## What you can do

- Maintain a centralized registry with standard fields, [custom fields](/en/guide/crm/customers), tags, and GPS addresses
- Detect and block duplicates by phone, email, or WhatsApp; merge records
- Import customers via CSV/Excel and export as CSV, Excel, or JSON
- View and move customers in [Kanban funnels](/en/guide/crm/funnels) with multiple funnels and stages
- Trigger automations on stage change with the `crm_stage_change` flow
- Filter by "Mine only", hide closed, and search by name
- Restrict visibility by team and hide sensitive data (GDPR/LGPD)
- Generate advanced reports with custom groupings (5-step assistant)
- Update cascading select field options and run bulk deletion

## How it connects

- **[Chat & Support](/en/features/chat)** — customer profile appears automatically in the side panel
- **[Automation & AI](/en/features/automation)** — agents and flows update CRM, funnel, and address
- **[UTM](/en/features/utm)** — campaign events recorded in funnel stages
- **[Medical module](/en/features/medical)** — patient shares the same customer record
