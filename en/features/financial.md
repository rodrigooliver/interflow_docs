# Financial Module

Track revenue, expenses, cash registers, and categories for internal operations — separate from customer billing and the Interflow subscription.

## What it's for

- Finance and accounting teams recording day-to-day transactions
- Owners and administrators configuring cash registers and operators
- Managers with read-only visibility into balance and cash flow
- Stores and restaurants integrating POS sales with financial control

::: info Different from Billing
This module manages the organization's **internal** finances. For contracts, installments, and Asaas integration with customers, use [Billing](/en/features/billing).
:::

## What you can do

- View a dashboard with consolidated balance, revenue, and expenses
- Record revenue or expense transactions with status: pending, paid, overdue, or cancelled
- Manage cash registers with operators and per-register permissions
- Organize revenue and expense categories, with subcategories
- Configure payment methods (credit, fees, installments)
- Optionally link transactions to a CRM customer
- Use AI suggestions for transaction categorization
- Export reports according to profile permissions

## How it connects

- **[POS](/en/features/pos)** — sales generate financial entries
- **[CRM & Customers](/en/features/crm)** — optional customer on transactions
- **[Billing](/en/features/billing)** — separate modules; Billing handles customer contracts
- **[Reports](/en/features/reports)** — exports according to `financial` profile
