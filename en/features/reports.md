# Reports & Analytics

Track operational performance with support metrics, message volume, teams, appointments, and CRM — with export and custom dashboards.

## What it's for

- Owners, admins, and managers who need a full view of operations
- Supervisors comparing teams, agents, and channels
- Management meetings with printable volume reports (PDF)
- Marketing analyzing conversion and customer source via advanced CRM reports

## What you can do

- Measure response time, resolution time, resolution rate, and satisfaction in support
- Generate [volume reports](/en/guide/reports/volume-reports) by agent and team, with charts and print
- Track team ranking, online time, and satisfaction by agent
- Analyze appointments: attendance, cancellation, and no-show
- Compare performance across support channels
- Create advanced CRM reports with groupings and CSV export
- Export as CSV, Excel, PDF, or JSON; schedule email delivery
- Build custom dashboards with widgets

::: info Permissions
Owners, admins, and managers see full metrics. Agents access personal metrics only.
:::
