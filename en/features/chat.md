# Chat & Support

The Interflow inbox brings together the tools agents use every day: conversations, filters, bulk actions, productivity features, and internal communication between agents.

## What it's for

- Agents who need to respond to customers quickly with full context
- Supervisors managing queues across multiple teams and channels
- Marketing and sales teams running segmented campaigns
- Managers standardizing closure reasons and support quality

## What you can do

- Work in the [chat interface](/en/guide/chat/interface) with three areas: conversation list, messages, and customer panel
- Filter by Mine, Unassigned, On hold, and Closed, plus custom filters (team, tag, channel, color)
- Take over, transfer, archive, close, and put conversations on hold; pause or resume active flows
- Run bulk actions: mark as read or resolved in batch
- Send [bulk messages](/en/guide/chat/bulk-messages) with a message, WAHA flow, or silent flow
- Organize conversations with [tags](/en/guide/chat/tags) and classify closures with [closure types](/en/guide/chat/closure-types) (trigger `attendance_closure` flow)
- Speed up replies with [message shortcuts](/en/guide/chat/message-shortcuts) (`/`) and the [text enhancer](/en/guide/chat/text-enhancer) (Ctrl+J)
- Add internal notes invisible to the customer
- Create tasks from the conversation — with the full thread, selected messages, or **Generate with AI**
- Use internal chat between agents (DM, groups, floating widget, audio)
- Start conversations quickly with the Quick WhatsApp Button

## How it connects

- **[Multichannel Support](/en/features/channels)** — source of all inbox conversations
- **[CRM & Customers](/en/features/crm)** — side panel with customer profile, tags, fields, and funnel
- **[Automation & AI](/en/features/automation)** — active flows, AI agents, and closure automations
- **[Tasks](/en/features/tasks)** — follow-ups and pending items created directly from support
