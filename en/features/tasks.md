# Tasks

Organize team work with projects, a visual board, calendar, personal view, and automations — from post-support follow-up to sprint management.

## What it's for

- Operations teams that need a Kanban board by status or priority
- Agents recording pending items and follow-ups after support sessions
- Project managers configuring templates, members, and automations
- Agile teams using simple sprints (backlog → review → done)

## What you can do

- Create projects with [templates](/en/guide/tasks/templates): Free, By status, By priority, or Simple sprint
- View tasks on a board (Kanban), list, or calendar, with drag-and-drop between columns
- Set status, priority, stage, assignees, due date, customer, checklist, and labels
- Manage members with Reader, Editor, or Administrator roles, with notifications
- Access **My Tasks** — unified view grouped by stage, project, or due date
- Configure [project automations](/en/guide/tasks/project-automations): sync status with column, follow-up, and silent flows
- Create tasks from chat — with the full conversation, selected messages, or **Generate with AI**
- Track performance indicators by person and period

## How it connects

- **[Chat & Support](/en/features/chat)** — tasks created directly from the customer conversation
- **[CRM & Customers](/en/features/crm)** — tasks linked to the customer record
- **[Automation & AI](/en/features/automation)** — flows create tasks; automations trigger silent flows
- **[Schedule](/en/features/schedule)** — complements operational follow-ups with appointments
