# Tasks

Projects with a visual board, list, calendar, My Tasks, templates, and automations.

## Highlights

- Projects with [templates](/en/guide/tasks/templates) (by status, priority, sprint, or free)
- Board with columns, cards, and drag and drop
- [My Tasks](/en/guide/tasks/) — unified view of what is yours
- Project [automations](/en/guide/tasks/project-automations)
- Create tasks from the customer conversation (whole chat or selected messages, with **Generate with AI**)
