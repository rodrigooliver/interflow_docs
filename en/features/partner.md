# Partner Portal

Manage resold organizations, custom plans, invoices, commissions, and Asaas wallet — all in a dedicated portal for platform partners.

## What it's for

- White-label resellers and agencies managing a customer portfolio
- Partners configuring Asaas wallet to receive revenue split
- Commission programs with invoices and automated payouts
- Network managers needing centralized visibility of child organizations

::: info Access
Available to partners with an accepted program and active status on the platform.
:::

## What you can do

- Manage resold organizations (create, configure, monitor)
- Create and edit custom plans for each child organization
- Track invoices and commissions generated by the partner program
- Configure Asaas wallet (`asaas_wallet_id`) for automatic revenue split
- View status and billing of managed organizations

## How it connects

- **[Billing](/en/features/billing)** — child organization billing can be managed by the partner
- **[Settings](/en/guide/settings/)** — subscription and configuration for each tenant organization
- Each tenant's Financial and Billing remain separate from the partner portal
