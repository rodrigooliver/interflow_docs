# WhatsApp group notifications

In busy groups, an alert on every message gets in the way. Each user can **turn off notifications** for a WhatsApp group — only for themselves. The group stays in the list, with history and the unread badge.

Changelog: [v2026.8.13](/en/changelog/2026/08/2026.8.13)

---

## What changes when you turn it off

| Still happens | Stops |
|---------------|--------|
| Group in the chat list | Push on phone or browser |
| Real-time messages | Sound when the app is open |
| Unread badge | — |

The preference is **personal**. Muting a group does not change alerts for teammates.

The default is **on**. It only changes when you turn it off.

---

## Where to change it

1. **Edit Group modal** (main) — click the group name in the header and use the **Notifications** switch at the top
2. **Group header** — bell button next to Participants
3. **Chat list** — group ⋮ menu → **Mute notifications** / **Unmute notifications**

Muted groups show a bell-off icon in the list and in the header.

---

## What this control does not cover

- Internal groups and direct chats between agents
- 1:1 customer conversations
- Muting the group for the **whole** organization at once
